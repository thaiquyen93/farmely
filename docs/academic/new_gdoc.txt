3.1.4 Non-Screen Functions
[
#
	Feature
	System Function
	Description
	1
	Authentication
	Authentication and Access Control
	Verifies credentials, account status, role permissions, and access to requested records.
	2
	Authentication
	Password Reset Email
	Generates a time-limited reset link and sends it to the registered email address.
	3
	Plot Rental
	Booking Validation
	Checks variety compatibility, minimum cultivation duration, plot availability, and conflicting rental schedules.
	4
	Plot Rental
	Price Calculation
	Calculates rental and care charges, including the first-month Starter Kit and additional service prices.
	5
	Plot Rental
	Reservation Expiration
	Releases unpaid reservations after 15 minutes, with a three-minute grace period for PAYMENT_PENDING.
	6
	Payment
	Mock Payment Processing
	Records mock payment results and updates the related contract or service request without duplicate processing.
	7
	Contract Management
	Contract Lifecycle Processing
	Confirms paid contracts, activates them on the start date, and completes unrenewed contracts on the end date.
	8
	Contract Management
	Renewal Processing
	Validates the seven-day renewal deadline and availability, then creates a linked continuation contract.
	9
	Contract Management
	Refund Calculation
	Calculates an 80% or 50% refund before the start date according to the cancellation policy.
	10
	Plot Management
	Maintenance Scheduling
	Blocks the plot for four days after an unrenewed contract ends before reopening its availability.
	11
	Care Management
	Automatic Task Generation
	Generates care tasks from standard processes for Assisted-growing contracts and successfully paid care services.
	12
	IoT Monitoring
	Sensor Simulation and Validation
	Generates readings every three hours, filters invalid temperature values, and records administrator warnings.
	13
	Weather Integration
	Weather Data Retrieval
	Retrieves local forecasts and returns an unavailable status when the weather service cannot respond.
	14
	Alert Management
	Alert Evaluation and Routing
	Detects prolonged threshold breaches and routes alerts or emergency tasks according to cultivation mode and operating hours.
	15
	Care Management
	Watering Task Postponement
	Postpones watering when heavy rain is forecast or soil humidity reaches at least 80%.
	16
	Task Management
	Overdue Task Detection
	Marks unfinished tasks as overdue after 23:59 and notifies the administrator the following morning.
	17
	Task Management
	Completion Validation and Diary Synchronization
	Requires task evidence before completion and automatically adds completed care activities to the Farm Diary.
	18
	Technical Support
	Intervention Application
	Associates expert-issued technical interventions with affected plots and their operational activities.
	19
	Communication
	Message Polling
	Retrieves authorized customer–expert and worker–expert messages through five-second HTTP polling.
	20
	AI Assistance
	Knowledge Indexing and Response Generation
	Indexes expert documents, retrieves relevant knowledge, and generates chatbot answers without memory between sessions.
	21
	Notification
	Notification Generation
	Creates in-app notifications for payments, contracts, assignments, alerts, services, and harvest events.
	22
	File Management
	Upload Validation
	Validates uploaded attachments and enforces image limits of 5 MB per file and ten photos per task.
	23
	System Monitoring
	Audit Logging
	Records relevant user and system actions with their actor, timestamp, and affected record.
	24
	Reporting
	Summary Aggregation
	Calculates dashboard summaries and reports from data accessible to the requesting user.
